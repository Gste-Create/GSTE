## Rights classes

- **CC0**: OpenScore Lieder explicitly releases its scores under CC0 and includes `LICENSE.txt`.
- **Public Domain — Mutopia**: the selected Mutopia work pages identify the editions as Public Domain. Mutopia states that its music may be downloaded, copied, distributed, modified, performed, and recorded.
- **Public Domain — MuseTrainer statement**: MuseTrainer describes its repository and library as “Public domain MusicXML files.” The repository does not present a separate CC0 licence, so these entries are accurately labelled Public Domain rather than CC0.
