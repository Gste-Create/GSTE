# GSTE 支援 / Support

## 中文
GSTE Beta V0.1 為公開測試版本，維護時間有限。尋求協助前請先閱讀 `docs/user/GETTING_STARTED.md`、`TROUBLESHOOTING.md` 與 `CURRENT_LIMITATIONS.md`。

回報 Bug 時請依 `docs/user/ISSUE_REPORT_GUIDE.md`，**優先提供 `PRIVACY_SAFE_DEBUG_REPORT.md`**。若專案涉及保密、商業合作或未公開作品，請勿直接上傳完整 `debug/` 或原始樂譜。

歡迎回報指彈吉他可演奏性、主旋律保留、伴奏、和聲、指法、把位、節奏與譜面問題；若可以，請標示小節或段落。目前不保證回覆時間或功能實作時程。

## English
GSTE Beta V0.1 is a public testing release with limited maintainer availability. Before requesting help, please read `docs/user/GETTING_STARTED.md`, `TROUBLESHOOTING.md`, and `CURRENT_LIMITATIONS.md`.

For bug reports, follow `docs/user/ISSUE_REPORT_GUIDE.md` and **share `PRIVACY_SAFE_DEBUG_REPORT.md` first**. Do not upload the full `debug/` folder or original score for confidential, commercial, or unpublished projects unless you have confirmed that sharing is permitted.

Feedback on guitar playability, melody preservation, accompaniment, harmony, fingering, position changes, rhythm, and notation is welcome. Measure or section references are helpful. Response times and implementation schedules are not guaranteed.
